How the Tracker Fills In Each Cell

The exact rules the FY27 Data Collection Tracker uses to decide TRUE, FALSE, In Progress, or blank for every column, and how the response rates are calculated.

The FY27 Data Collection Tracker rebuilds every hour from the SY26-27 Participant Roster and the surveys. This page lists the rules it follows, column by column. For why a submitted survey might not be matched to a person, see Why Doesn’t the Tracker Count My Participants?

What the cell values mean

Value Meaning Counts toward the response rate?
TRUE A finished response was matched to this person Yes, as done
FALSE This person needs it and nothing has been matched yet Yes, as not done
In Progress A response was started but not submitted. Click it to open the response Yes, as not done
A number Coaching log days, minutes, or Participant Feedback responses See Response rates
Blank This person isn’t expected to complete it No. Blank cells are left out of the rate entirely

Blank does not mean “missing”. It means the tracker isn’t counting that column for that person, so they don’t help or hurt the rate.

Who gets a row

  • Everyone on the SY26-27 Participant Roster whose Participant Status is Current/Active or Completed/Cycled Out and who has a Participant Email.
  • If Updated Participant Name or Updated Participant Email is filled in on the roster, that value is used instead of the original.
  • A person gets one row per content area and per school listed on the roster. A teacher coached in Math and ELA has two rows. That’s on purpose, so each subject’s project sees them.

When a cell is blank

Two things on the roster decide whether a column applies to a person: the “Needed?” columns and the Participant Role.

The “Needed?” columns

Each roster item has four Yes/No columns. “No” always blanks the matching tracker columns, whatever the role.

Roster column Tracker columns it controls
Ed Survey Needed? Diagnostic Educator Survey, Follow-up Educator Survey
BOY/EOY Observations Needed? Baseline observation, End of year observation
MOY Observations/Student Work Needed? Middle of year observation, Middle of Year Student Work
BOY/EOY Student Work Needed? Baseline Student Work, Follow-up Student Work

Participant Role

These roles are treated as non-teachers: School leader or administrator, District leader or administrator, Community-based organization leader, Coach (plus the older FY26 labels School Leader, District Leader, School-based admin, and School-based coach). “Coach, Teacher/specialist” counts as a teacher.

For a non-teacher, the observation, student work, and student survey columns show TRUE or In Progress if they have a response, and blank otherwise. They never show FALSE, even when the “Needed?” column says Yes. So an unobserved leader or coach is not counted as missing.

Role does not affect the educator survey, coaching log, or Participant Feedback columns.

Service Type

If Service Type is exactly PL, and the person has no coaching log time, the two coaching log columns are blank instead of FALSE. “Coaching, PL” is treated as coaching.

Column by column

The order below is the order the tracker checks things in. The first rule that applies wins.

Diagnostic Educator Survey

  1. Ed Survey Needed? = No → blank
  2. A finished Educator Survey response marked as pre, from the Participant Email or Secondary Participant Email → TRUE
  3. A started but unsubmitted response from either email → In Progress
  4. Otherwise → FALSE

Follow-up Educator Survey

  1. Ed Survey Needed? = No → blank
  2. A finished response marked as post, from either email → TRUE
  3. Otherwise → FALSE (no In Progress link for the follow-up)

What counts as pre and post: a person’s first response before April 1, 2027 is pre. Their second response, or any response on or after April 1, 2027, is post. If a person’s first response is on or after April 1, it’s still pre as long as a second response comes at least a month later. If a project has its own Educator Survey cutoff later than April 1, every response before that cutoff is pre.

Baseline, Middle of Year, and End of Year Observation

  1. The matching “Needed?” column = No → blank
  2. A finished Classroom Observation (IPG) for this round where the teacher picked matches the roster Participant Name → TRUE
  3. A started but unsubmitted observation for this round → In Progress
  4. Non-teacher role → blank
  5. Otherwise → FALSE

The round is whatever the coach picked in the survey: “Baseline (first observation of the year)”, “Mid-year (middle of service, if applicable)”, or “End of year (last observation of the year)”. Names are matched exactly, after straightening apostrophes and extra spaces. A short list of 31 observations from late SY25-26 (CUNY/UA, D9, D13, D75) is also credited this year.

Baseline, Middle of Year, and Follow-up Student Work

  1. The matching “Needed?” column = No → blank
  2. A finished Student Work submission for this round where the teacher picked, or the co-teacher named, matches the roster Participant Name → TRUE
  3. A finished submission for this round from the roster Participant Email → TRUE
  4. Non-teacher role → blank
  5. Otherwise → FALSE

Only submitted responses are read for Student Work, so these columns never show In Progress.

Rounds: Baseline is “Baseline (first submission of the year)” or “Assets & Needs Assessment”. Middle of Year is “Mid-year (middle of service, if applicable)”. If a teacher’s second submission of the year is also marked Baseline, it counts as Middle of Year too. Follow-up is “End of year (last submission of the year)”.

Submissions the graders skipped because the files were blank, empty, or broken don’t count for Baseline or Middle of Year.

Coaching Log Completion

Shows the number of different days the person appears on the FY27 Coaching Log, as a 1:1 coachee, a group coaching participant, or a school admin meeting attendee. If any of those days were group days, it shows the split, e.g. 3 (2 1:1, 1 group). A day with both a 1:1 and a group session counts once, as a 1:1 day.

  • Logs with a no-coaching reason (for example “Partner Canceled”) don’t count.
  • Names are matched to the roster Participant Name, ignoring capitalization, apostrophe style, and extra spaces.
  • Not on any log → FALSE, unless Service Type is exactly PL (then blank).

Coaching Log Total Time

Total minutes across 1:1 coaching, group coaching, micro PL, and school admin meetings, from the same logs. Not on any log → FALSE, or blank if Service Type is exactly PL.

Participant Feedback

The number of finished Participant Feedback responses from the person’s Participant Email, or FALSE if there are none. There’s no In Progress link for this survey.

Baseline and Follow-up Student Survey

Matched on the teacher’s email on the Student Survey.

  1. Non-teacher role → blank
  2. The teacher has any finished student responses → Baseline TRUE
  3. The teacher has finished student responses 60 or more days after their first one → Follow-up TRUE
  4. Otherwise → FALSE

Student Assessment Data

Set per project, not per person: TRUE for everyone at a site when the BOY Ss Assessment Data Status on the partnerships board is filled in. That column isn’t on the FY27 board right now, so this shows FALSE for everyone until it comes back.

Response rates

Row 2 of every site tab shows that tab’s rates. Blank cells never count.

Columns Rate
Educator Survey, Observations, Student Work, Student Survey TRUE ÷ (TRUE + FALSE + In Progress)
Coaching Log Completion, Participant Feedback People with a number ÷ (people with a number + FALSE)
Coaching Log Total Time Average minutes per person with a number

The Project Overview tab copies row 2 from each site tab. Its Org Total row is the average of the project rates, so every project counts equally regardless of size.

Site tabs

  • Each site has its own tab that pulls its rows live from the Tracker tab.
  • NY_D9, D11, D12, D13, D25, and D75 are split into Math and ELA tabs. Math is “Math” or “SpEd Math”; everything else, including a blank content area, goes to ELA.
  • In NY_D7, D11, D12, D13, D16, and D25, participants whose Grade Level is exactly Pre-K go to a separate ECE tab and are left out of the Math and ELA tabs.

Something looks wrong?

Check the roster first: the status, email, role, and “Needed?” columns decide most of what the tracker shows. Mary Kate Neal manages the roster. If the roster looks right and the cell still doesn’t, email Duncan Gates with the participant’s name, site, and the column.
